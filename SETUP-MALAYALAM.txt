VR1 DIGITAL KURI – PORTAL STARTER

Included:
- index.html: responsive Green/Gold public portal preview
- assets/vr1-logo.jpeg: logo supplied by user
- firestore.rules.template: deny-all starter template (not production-ready)

Planned deployment architecture:
1. Public landing page: group overview, published winner card, login entry only.
2. Members: authenticated shared member access (Firebase Auth/backend implementation); read-only payment status, totals, notices, winner history.
3. Admin: Firebase Authentication with admin-only permission; member/payment editing, draw publishing, announcements, credential management.
4. Database: Firestore with restrictive rules; private member/payment records must not be publicly readable.
5. WhatsApp: manual share first; automated messages require WhatsApp Business API/provider, consent, templates and backend scheduling.
6. Responsive layout: mobile, tablet, desktop.

This is a visual/configuration starter, not a deployed portal. Login, Firebase, draw logic,
member data, security policy and automatic WhatsApp messages still require configuration.
Never place shared passwords or secrets in HTML/client-side JavaScript.
